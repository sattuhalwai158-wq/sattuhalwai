# N-Sattu Cuisine Light Luxury Redesign

## Goal
Transform the existing catering website from its dark palace styling into a bright, bridal, editorial experience while preserving the working multi-page structure and keeping WhatsApp intentionally deferred.

## Visual direction
- Replace dark surfaces with crisp white, pearl ivory, cream, and blush sections.
- Use Rani pink for primary actions and highlights, antique gold for fine borders and decorative details, charcoal for headings, and slate for body copy.
- Keep the existing regal serif and premium sans-serif pairing, with soft shadows, arched photography, clean spacing, and restrained motion.

## Page updates
- Add a slim contact/location strip and redesign the sticky navigation as a white glass bar with an elevated royal seal identity.
- Recompose Home into a light editorial journey: immersive banquet opening, compact enquiry panel, founder welcome, four experiences, curated dishes, milestones, destination coverage, setup gallery, reviews, FAQ preview, and a strong call-based closing invitation.
- Restyle Heritage, Menus, Luxury Setups, Reviews, Booking, and legal pages to use the same light luxury system without changing their core interactions.
- Expand the footer into a four-column light layout with links, specialties, contact details, and social links.

## Interactions
- Keep menu filters, dish details, setup lightbox, FAQ accordion, estimator, booking steps, and mobile navigation operational.
- Keep all WhatsApp buttons and links disabled/deferred; enquiries continue through direct phone calls.
- Preserve responsive layouts, keyboard access, validation, reduced-motion support, and per-page metadata.

## Verification
- Check all pages and key interactions on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm the light theme has no unintended dark page or card surfaces, no clipped text, and no preview errors.
